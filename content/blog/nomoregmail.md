I've been using Gmail for a really long time now, I think that's everyone default email address at this point. I've been aware about Google's data scraping for some time now, which has practically been the norm nowadays.

A while back, I've heard of a mail service called [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/). I don't remember who introduced it to me, but the main talking points is that your email remains encrypted no matter what.

Natrually, I was interested, I did a trial run to it, and it was your typical mail service, with the added bonus of proper encryption.

I've been wanting to transfer my email, especially my domain email (akmijares.ca) to it, however a few things were stopping me, especially for my domain:
1. I was still under the "contract" that Google forces you to sign up with when you get Google Workspace.
2. Some of the services sign into Google with my domain (i.e., Tailscale).
3. Laziness.

Until, Google started to implement AI onto their email platform. Now, I don't have a huge problem with it, heck I sometimes use it from time to time as well. However, [when they launched an AI scraper to everyone's emails, and when disabling it disables everything else](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2025/11/gmail-is-reading-your-emails-and-attachments-to-train-its-ai-unless-you-turn-it-off), I was livid. At that point, I told myself, might as well do it.

So, I signed up properly for Proton Mail, bought a 1 year subscription and started their tools to transfer my mails to Proton.

Apparently it was that easy.

Except for my domain emails.

Now, apparently cancelling Google Workspace, I can either:
Let it lapse until renewal, and cancel then or cancel right now, and pay the remaining fees in one swoop.

Now it was their business model to do so, I think most companies do it as well. So I just sait screw it, might as well cancel. Luckily I can still keep the "Sign in with Google" for the websites that use it.

So after about a days worth of transferring my emails, and unsubscribing to newsletters (apparently, I have lots of them), I am now using Proton Mail.

Was it worth it? Yes, at least that's the first step in [DeGoogle](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-de-google). I won't be able to fully De-Google, but at least I'm taking steps to take back my privacy.
